YuNet's existing max-side-640 detected row substitutes for Intel's face detector and five-landmark regressor; the row is the frozen one in the prior combined-crop manifest. The decoded BGR photo is cut at YuNet XYWH bounds truncated to integers and clipped to the image. Its five absolute landmarks are normalized to that integer ROI. The official demo's SVD similarity warp calls `cv2.warpAffine` with **only** `cv2.WARP_INVERSE_MAP`: the interpolation bits are zero, so the warp uses **nearest-neighbor**. The subsequent [resize](https://github.com/openvinotoolkit/open_model_zoo/blob/a6946b6d6ce42cbf4278df20275fab199655fc7d/demos/face_recognition_demo/python/utils.py) to 128×128 uses `cv2.INTER_LINEAR`, followed by BGR→NCHW and float32 cast **without an external mean or scale**. Embeddings are raw 256-value output; future gallery comparison would use within-model cosine and a new model-specific frozen threshold, never reuse SFace/Ghost thresholds.
