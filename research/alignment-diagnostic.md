# H9 alignment transfer diagnostic (development only)

H9 asked whether YuNet-specific alignment was the main reason H7 regular-dot edits transferred poorly to an independently aligned ArcFace development recognizer. This diagnostic used only the two previously used FRLL development sources (`frll-001`, `frll-003`) and the four frozen H7 JPEG exports. It performed no image optimization, parameter search, held-out model evaluation, or new identity selection.

The protocol was frozen before inference. For each person, the four clean gallery views were embedded once with the [pinned official InsightFace SCRFD and ArcFaceONNX pipeline](https://github.com/deepinsight/insightface/tree/1480e705287bc5d59f923b46c260ec6e3e4150f6). Each edited JPEG was then queried three ways against **that same canonical gallery**: (1) official SCRFD landmarks and `face_align.norm_crop`; (2) fresh YuNet landmarks with the same official `norm_crop` and ArcFace network; (3) YuNet landmarks detected on the clean source, applied to the edit. The latter two are counterfactual alignment probes, **not official ArcFace scores or independent transfer evidence**. The ArcFace threshold remained its frozen development-calibration value, 0.2376004863; no thresholds or references were refit.

The predeclared criterion for considering a later H9 jitter experiment was: all four edits valid; median reduction in maximum gallery cosine from canonical to fresh-YuNet alignment at least 0.05 across the four; and a positive median reduction for each person. Detection or selection failure would be inconclusive. Exact source/gallery/JPEG hashes, the H7 executed source, the ArcFace adapter and official assets, and the original and copied YuNet/SFace ONNX files were verified before model calls. The protocol and executed script were saved outside Git.

All four queries were valid and all maximum cosines remained above the ArcFace development match threshold. The median canonical-to-fresh reduction was **0.0150**, below 0.05; two edits worsened. Clean-source YuNet versus SCRFD had aligned-frame p95 displacement of 4.40 px for 001 and 3.50 px for 003. Thus detector alignment differs visibly in pixel space, but this swap did not explain enough of the observed recognition match to justify H9 jitter optimization under the frozen criterion. It does not rule out every possible alignment-diversity method.

The four canonical scores reproduced the earlier frozen H7 ArcFace export scores within 2.9×10⁻⁸. The complete machine output includes all five landmarks, source-to-112 affine maps, clean-to-edit shifts, frame-displacement percentiles, per-reference scores, and crop comparisons.
