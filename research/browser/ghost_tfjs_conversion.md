The probe's diagnostic limits were declared before browser testing: author unit-vector cosine ≥0.999 and maximum unit-vector absolute difference ≤0.02; for a float32 clone, raw output maximum absolute difference ≤0.001 and input-gradient cosine ≥0.99 against the frozen float32 Python reference. All gradients must be finite and nonzero. Three highest-gradient coordinates get central finite differences at epsilon 0.01; at least two must agree within absolute 1e-5 or relative 25%. These are feasibility diagnostics, not application validation. A backend, load, inference, or gradient failure is reported as a failure, without changing limits after inspection.
